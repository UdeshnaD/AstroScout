# Planet image sources

These are bundled reference photographs, not live views or simulated appearances at the selected observing time.

- Moon: NASA/JPL, PIA00405.
- Mercury: NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution of Washington, PIA12371, true and enhanced colour composite.
- Venus: NASA/JPL, PIA00271.
- Mars: NASA/JPL, PIA00407.
- Jupiter: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI; Ricardo Hueso, Imke de Pater, Thierry Fouchet, Leigh Fletcher, Michael Wong, Joseph DePasquale. Webb NIRCam enhanced-colour image: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/jupiter-nircam-image/
- Saturn: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute, PIA21345.
- Uranus: NASA/JPL, PIA18182.
- Neptune: NASA/JPL, PIA01492.
- Pluto: NASA/JHUAPL/Southwest Research Institute, PIA19952.

PIA images were retrieved from NASA's image library. Mercury was retrieved from https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/mercury-in-true-and-enhanced-color/.
